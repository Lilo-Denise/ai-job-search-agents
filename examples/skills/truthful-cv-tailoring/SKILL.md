# Truthful CV tailoring (shortened example)

## Steps

1. **Check the job is live.** Open the post. If it is closed, mark the role Dead and stop.
2. **Read the three sources.** Master CV, master profile, story bank. Nothing else counts as evidence.
3. **Fit analysis.** List the post's must-have requirements. For each, quote the matching evidence or write "gap". Score on the fixed rubric. Show this to the candidate.
4. **Pick the base CV** for this role type.
5. **Edit as tracked changes.** Reword real achievements in the post's language. Do not add tools, numbers, titles or scope that the sources do not contain.
6. **Check the page.** Render to PDF and confirm one page with no single-word last lines.
7. **Deliver** the tracked-changes file path and a short list of what changed and why.

## Never

- Copy a requirement from the job post into the CV as if it were experience.
- Round numbers up or turn "contributed to" into "led".
- Hide a gap. Name it in the fit analysis instead.
